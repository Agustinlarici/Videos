## Intent

A 15-second promo for LRC IT Solutions (lrc-solutions.com), embedded on the
company's own site. Concept: **Chiaro** — real production/office texture
(a blinking gauge, a stack of paper checklists, a cluttered spreadsheet)
visually resolves, piece by piece, into the clean product dashboard UI.
The video closes on the brand tagline card — "Il tuo processo. La nostra
priorità." — over the resolved dashboard, landing on the wordmark. No
voiceover; the piece is carried by texture, motion, and on-screen Italian
type. Tone: confident, precise, no stock-corporate gloss.

## Customizations

- Do not use the "+24%" efficiency stat as the ending payoff (user declined
  it explicitly) — the resolve target is the tagline card + wordmark
  instead, not a KPI count-up.
- No narration/voiceover track; text-and-motion only.
